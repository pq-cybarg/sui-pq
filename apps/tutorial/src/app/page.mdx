## What this is

This tutorial is the long-form counterpart to the `sui-gen` workspace. The workspace
is the monorepo (Move packages, TypeScript SDKs, demo apps); this tutorial walks
through the *why* of each piece in the order a beginner would build them.

Every page includes:

- **Concept** — what the primitive is and why it exists.
- **Code** — the minimum useful snippet, in TypeScript or Move.
- **Try it** — a button that exercises the feature against live Sui testnet using
  your wallet.

## How to follow along

You can read the entire thing without doing anything — every demo also points at a
canonical artifact already deployed on testnet. To run the demos that mutate state,
you'll need:

- Any Sui wallet ([Slush](https://slush.app), Suiet, Phantom, Nightly, or OKX)
- A bit of testnet SUI for gas (free, [step 1](/setup) shows how)

## Why Sui

Sui's distinguishing primitive is the **object**. Every on-chain thing —
NFTs, coins, balances, app state — is a typed object with explicit ownership and
abilities (`key`, `store`, `copy`, `drop`). This makes parallel execution trivial,
gas predictable, and the developer experience way closer to ORM-style data than
EVM-style storage slots.

The Move 2024 edition (used throughout this workspace) gives you method syntax,
struct fields, and explicit visibility — read your Sui Move and it looks like
normal modern code.
